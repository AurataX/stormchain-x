# Demonstration contract (Phase 5)

0:00–1:00: identify the synthetic coastal district and the decision problem.
Select an asset without reports; explain that unknown does not mean destroyed.

1:00–2:00: inspect conflicting substation evidence and generate a feasible
initial plan. Show actual budget, crew allocation, and road prerequisites.

2:00–3:15: submit a timestamped simulated report closing Highway 101. Show
the persisted observation and stale-plan indicator. Recalculate once.

3:15–4:30: compare the stored plans. Explain changed access constraints,
alternate routes, and any verification action that the computed plan selects.
Do not force a scripted action if the optimizer chooses another feasible result.

4:30–5:00: show the grounded explanation and measured metrics. Identify model
assumptions and synthetic provenance. Keep a local, rehearsed fallback available.

Demonstration reset must be explicit and isolated from saved evidence. Phase 1
bootstrap preserves existing observations and is not a reset command.
